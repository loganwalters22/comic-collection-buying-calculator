02 · Set your buying goals

iUse your selling platform’s combined selling and payment-processing rate. Enter 0 for a fee-free sale. Rates vary; check your platform’s current terms. Put fixed fees in total selling costs.
iOne total for the entire collection: seller-paid shipping, packing, grading, travel, and other expenses. Include only costs you expect to pay. Do not include the purchase price or percentage selling fees again.
iChoose how much of gross sales you want to keep as profit after buying the collection and paying costs. At 30%, $1,000 in sales means $300 profit. Use more cushion for uncertain values, slow sales, or condition risk. The 30% starting value is an example, not a recommended industry rate. Margin is not markup or return on your purchase cost.

04 · Know your buying limits

Compare purchase-price ceilings before negotiating. These are amounts you pay for the collection, not prices you sell it for.

Cautious and target limits keep your chosen profit margin. Break-even keeps no profit. Fees change with resale value; total selling costs stay fixed. Negative ceilings display as $0 with a warning.

All amounts USD. Calculated with full precision; displayed amounts rounded to whole dollars. Estimates, not appraisals. No live pricing. No accounts or cloud collection storage.
